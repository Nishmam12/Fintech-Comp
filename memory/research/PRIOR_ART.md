# Prior Art and Research Notes

Use this page to separate sources from our interpretation. Every material factual claim should include source title, publisher, URL, access date, relevant claim and confidence/limitation.

## Research questions
- Which existing agent-payment protocols handle signed mandates, delegation, user consent and payment authorization?
- Which systems provide transactional/capability-based limits across hierarchies of autonomous agents?
- What prior work exists on model routing, cascade inference, selective prediction and cost-sensitive escalation?
- What existing systems separate LLM-proposed actions from deterministic policy/transaction execution?
- What evaluation protocols measure financial constraint adherence, cost per correct decision and authorization safety?
- What current competition categories, deadlines, deliverables and judging criteria apply?

## Novelty discipline
Do not write “first,” “never done before,” or “no one has built this” without a systematic prior-art review. Frame the project contribution as a testable hypothesis until evidence supports stronger language.
